---
name: research
description: "Investigate a requested question against repository evidence and authoritative primary sources; produce supported findings and explicit uncertainty."
metadata:
  version: 0.1.0-pilot.1
---

Define the question and decision the evidence must support. Inspect existing repository notes and primary sources that own each material claim. Record source version/date where relevant, distinguish observation from inference, and investigate contradictions.

Write findings in the project's established notes location, with direct claim-to-source links, limitations, unresolved questions, and the practical implication for the requested decision. Complete when material claims are supported or explicitly marked unknown. Collect results synchronously within the current run; background completion is not a handoff. A required unavailable research method is an explicit blocker, not an implied permission to substitute.
